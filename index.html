About HOWLERS

A different breed.

HOWLERS is a Pakistani streetwear brand shaped by underground streetwear and biker culture. Bold, minimal and made to stand apart.

The wolf

Sharp lines. One emblem.

The angular wolf is the mark behind everything we make. It sits small on the chest and again on the back, beneath the HOWLERS lettering and the line "A different breed."

VOL. 2

We are back.

VOL. 2 is a new chapter of HOWLERS. It starts with oversized hoodies in two colourways: black with crimson graphics, and ivory with dusty pink graphics. Both are unisex.

Lightning-bolt graphics run down the sleeves and across the hood, and the back carries the full HOWLERS lettering.
